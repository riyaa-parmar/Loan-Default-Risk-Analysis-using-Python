# Loan Default Risk Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is based on statistical and probability analysis of loan applications.

The main aim of this project is to study customer income, loan amount, credit score, and default status using Python.

The project covers basic statistics, probability, distributions, data visualization, and linear algebra.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains around 5,000 loan application records.

### Features:

- `Customer_ID` – Unique ID of each customer
- `Age` – Age of the customer
- `Income` – Customer's income
- `Loan_Amount` – Amount of loan applied for
- `Credit_Score` – Credit score of the customer
- `Loan_Term (months)` – Loan duration in months
- `Default_Status` – Whether the customer defaulted or not (Yes/No)

## 📚 Project Work

### Part A – Theory

The theory section covers:

1. Mean, Median and Mode
2. Variance and Standard Deviation
3. Random Variable
4. Conditional Probability
5. Bayes' Theorem
6. Empirical and Theoretical Probability
7. Poisson Distribution
8. Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

The complete theory answers are available in the `Theory` folder.

### Part B – Practical

#### Step 1: Central Tendency & Dispersion

- Mean of Income
- Median of Income
- Mode of Income
- Range of Loan Amount
- Variance of Loan Amount
- Standard Deviation of Loan Amount

#### Step 2: Probability & Events

- Probability of loan default
- Credit Score categories
- Default Status vs Credit Score contingency table
- Conditional probability:
  `P(Default | Credit Score < 600)`

#### Step 3: Distributions & Visualization

- Credit Score histogram
- Gaussian curve
- Skewness of Loan Amount
- Kurtosis of Loan Amount
- Q-Q Plot of Income

#### Step 4: Linear Algebra

Using the first five customers:

- Income and Loan Amount as vectors
- Dot product of two customer vectors
- Norm of Customer 2
- Angle between Customer 1 and Customer 2
